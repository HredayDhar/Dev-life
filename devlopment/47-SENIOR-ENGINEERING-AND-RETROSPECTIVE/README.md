# 47 — Senior Engineering and Retrospective

This phase focuses on advanced engineering practices that come with experience and leadership, including engineering judgment, trade-off analysis, technical leadership, design reviews, architecture decisions, mentoring, and retrospectives. These practices help engineers grow from individual contributors to technical leaders who can guide teams, make sound decisions, and continuously improve both products and processes.

Effective senior engineering combines deep technical expertise with business acumen, communication skills, and the ability to mentor others. Retrospectives provide structured opportunities for teams to reflect on what worked, what didn't, and how to improve, creating a cycle of continuous learning and adaptation.

The topics in this phase build upon all previous phases, applying maintenance, refactoring, release practices, and feedback mechanisms with the wisdom that comes from experience and a broader systems perspective.
