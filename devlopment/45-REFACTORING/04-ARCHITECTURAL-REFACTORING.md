# 04 — Architectural Refactoring

## 1. What Is This?

Architectural refactoring involves restructuring the fundamental structure, organization, or interaction patterns of a software system without changing its external behavior. It focuses on improving non-functional qualities such as scalability, maintainability, modularity, and performance by altering the system's architecture—like changing layers, extracting services, or modifying communication patterns—while preserving functionality.
