# 13 — ASSUMPTIONS

This phase focuses on identifying, documenting, validating, and managing assumptions throughout the software development lifecycle. Assumptions are beliefs or statements taken for granted as true without proof or demonstration. Proper assumption management is critical for risk mitigation, decision-making, and project success.

## Files in this Phase:
- 01-ASSUMPTIONS.md — Fundamentals of assumptions in software engineering
- 02-VALIDATING-ASSUMPTIONS.md — Techniques for testing and validating assumptions
- 03-ASSUMPTION-RISKS.md — Managing risks associated with incorrect assumptions

Effective assumption management helps prevent costly mistakes, identifies knowledge gaps, and supports evidence-based decision making throughout the project lifecycle.
