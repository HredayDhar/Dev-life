# 15 — RISK MANAGEMENT

This phase focuses on identifying, analyzing, prioritizing, mitigating, and monitoring risks throughout the software development lifecycle. Effective risk management is essential for preventing project failures, minimizing negative impacts, and ensuring successful delivery.

## Files in this Phase:
- 01-RISK-IDENTIFICATION.md — Techniques for discovering and documenting potential risks
- 02-RISK-ANALYSIS.md — Methods for assessing risk probability, impact, and exposure
- 03-RISK-PRIORITIZATION.md — Approaches for ranking risks based on severity and urgency
- 04-RISK-MITIGATION.md — Strategies for reducing, transferring, accepting, or avoiding risks
- 05-RISK-REGISTER.md — Tools and practices for tracking and monitoring risks over time

Proper risk management helps teams anticipate problems, allocate resources effectively, and make informed decisions throughout the project lifecycle.
