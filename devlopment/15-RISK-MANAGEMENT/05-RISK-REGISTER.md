# 15-05 — RISK REGISTER

## 1. What Is This?

A risk register is a living document that serves as a centralized repository for all identified risks, their characteristics, current status, and mitigation efforts. It typically includes risk descriptions, categories, probability and impact assessments, risk scores, owners, mitigation strategies, status updates, and review dates. The risk register is the primary tool for tracking and monitoring risks throughout the project lifecycle, ensuring that risks are not forgotten and that mitigation efforts are effective.

## 2. Why Does It Matter?

The risk register matters because it:
- Provides a single source of truth for all project risks
- Enables consistent risk tracking and monitoring over time
- Facilitates regular risk reviews and updates
